SECTION 1: CARDIOVASCULAR HEALTH
Normal blood pressure is defined as <120/80 mmHg. Hypertension (Stage 1) is 130-139/80-89 mmHg. 
Causal links: High sodium intake increases fluid retention, leading to elevated systolic pressure.

SECTION 2: METABOLIC & DIABETES
Glucose management is vital for overall metabolic health. Normal fasting glucose is <100 mg/dL. 
A causal relationship exists between visceral adiposity and insulin resistance (Type 2 Diabetes).

SECTION 3: NUTRITION & WELLNESS
Adults require 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity per week. 
Nutrition: A Mediterranean-style diet is causally associated with reduced inflammation 
and lower risk of neurodegenerative diseases.

SECTION 4: RESPIRATORY & GENERAL 
Dyspnea (shortness of breath) can be caused by cardiac or pulmonary issues. 
Diagnostic differentiation is required to determine if the cause is heart failure or COPD.
